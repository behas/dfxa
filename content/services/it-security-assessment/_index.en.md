An IT security assessment reviews the architecture, configuration and operation of IT systems for vulnerabilities and derives hardening measures. It can also answer whether the security measures in place at a given time corresponded to the state of the art.

## Typical questions

- Does the IT infrastructure correspond to the state of the art?
- Which vulnerabilities enabled or facilitated an incident?
- Which measures reduce the risk of future attacks?
