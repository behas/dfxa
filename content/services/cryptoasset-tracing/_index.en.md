Cryptoasset tracing (cryptocurrency forensics) follows fund flows on blockchains such as Bitcoin or Ethereum to establish where funds came from and where they were transferred to. The analysis relies on publicly available transaction data and on blockchain analysis methods.

## Typical questions

- Where were cryptoassets transferred to after a fraud or theft?
- Can addresses be attributed to a person, a trading platform or a service?
- At which cryptoasset exchanges can funds be frozen or information be requested?
