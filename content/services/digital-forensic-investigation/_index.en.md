A digital forensic investigation is the methodical preservation and analysis of digital traces on computers, mobile devices, servers or cloud services in order to establish the facts of a matter. The results are documented so that they can be used in court proceedings.

## Typical questions

- How did an incident unfold, and which systems and accounts were affected?
- Were data copied, modified or deleted without authorisation?
- Which traces did a specific person or piece of software leave on a system?
