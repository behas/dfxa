Incident response is the structured handling of an IT security incident, for example a ransomware attack or the compromise of user accounts. It comprises containment, analysis, eradication and recovery, as well as coordinating crisis management and alignment with executive management.

## Typical questions

- How can an ongoing attack be contained?
- Which systems and data are affected, and since when?
- Which steps need to be documented for recovery and for statutory notification obligations?
